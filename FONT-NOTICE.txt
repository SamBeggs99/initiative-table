Bundled typefaces
=================

The three typefaces in `public/fonts/` are self-hosted (rather than loaded from
Google Fonts) so that the app boots with no network connection. Each is licensed
under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, which permits redistribution and
bundling. Full licence text: https://openfontlicense.org

  IBM Plex Sans        Copyright (c) 2017 IBM Corp.
                       https://github.com/IBM/plex
                       Used for all UI text.

  JetBrains Mono       Copyright (c) 2020 The JetBrains Mono Project Authors
                       https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono
                       Used for HP, AC, initiative and other tabular figures.

  Fraunces             Copyright (c) 2019 The Fraunces Project Authors
                       https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces
                       Used for display headings only.

Each family ships as one variable WOFF2 per Unicode subset (latin, latin-ext),
subsetted by Google Fonts. Files were retrieved from fonts.gstatic.com; the
upstream sources above are authoritative. To refresh, re-download the latin and
latin-ext WOFF2 for each family and keep the filenames in `src/index.css`.
